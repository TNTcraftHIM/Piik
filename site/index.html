Quick start使用教程

Start your first room.开始第一次分享。

Download the App to start sharing, or open Piik online and use it right in your browser.下载 Piik App，开始分享；也可以直接打开在线版，用浏览器开房间。

Use online在线版Piik AppPiik AppExisting site已有站点Join as a viewer加入房间
01Share online直接在线分享Open in your browser and start sharing打开浏览器就能开房间、发邀请
  1. Open Piik online.打开在线版。

    Visit demo.piik.tv on a computer or phone. 用电脑或手机浏览器打开 demo.piik.tv。

  2. Share a screen and copy its invitation.分享画面，复制邀请。

    Select Start sharing. On a computer, use Browser to choose a screen, window or tab. You can also choose Camera on a supported computer or phone browser. Once the preview appears, select Copy invite link.点击开始分享。电脑上可通过浏览器选择屏幕、窗口或标签页； 电脑和手机也可选择摄像头（需浏览器支持）。预览出现后，点击复制邀请链接。

  3. Send the invitation to your friends.把邀请发给朋友。

    Friends can open the link on a phone or computer. Select Play if playback does not start automatically.朋友用手机或电脑打开链接即可观看。如果没有自动播放，点击播放。

  4. End your share.结束分享。

    When you are finished, select Stop sharing on the host page.结束时，在房主页面点击停止分享。

About this online site在线版说明

This site uses direct device-to-device connections (P2P), with media forwarding disabled. Restrictive networks may open the page but block the video.在线版仅使用设备间直连（P2P），未启用媒体转发。 在受限网络下，可能出现页面能打开、画面无法连接的情况。

Self-hosted Piik sites can enable media forwarding (SFU) as a fallback when a direct connection cannot be established.自行部署的 Piik 站点可以启用媒体转发（SFU），为直连失败提供兜底。

02Share with Piik App用 Piik App 分享Download, open and create a room下载后直接开房间
  1. Download Piik App.下载 Piik App。

    Download Piik App. Extract the desktop package or install the Android APK. On iPhone or iPad, open Piik in Safari.下载 Piik App。电脑上解压后打开，安卓设备安装 APK。iPhone 或 iPad 请用 Safari 打开在线版。

    Windows
    Open 打开 piik-app.exe
    macOS
    Open 打开 Piik App.app
    Linux
    Run 运行 ./piik-app
    Mobile手机／平板
    Open Piik after installation安装后打开 Piik
  2. Choose Public invite, then Open Piik.选择「公网邀请」，点击「进入 Piik」。

    The launcher opens in your system browser. Public invite creates a temporary Internet address for friends outside your network. Choose Local room for the same network; to use an existing site, see guide 03.启动页会在系统浏览器里打开。「公网邀请」会生成临时公网地址，方便不在同一网络的朋友加入。同一局域网可选「本地房间」；搭配已有站点使用见教程 03。

  3. Start sharing and choose a source.点击「开始分享」，选择来源。

    Choose Browser, Camera, Apps / Windows or Screens where available. Select the picture and audio you want to share.按可用选项选择浏览器、摄像头、程序 / 窗口或屏幕，再选择具体画面和声音。

  4. Copy the invitation and keep the App open.复制邀请，并保持 App 运行。

    Check the preview and select Copy invite link. Keep both the App and sharing tab open until you finish.确认预览后点击复制邀请链接。分享期间保持 App 和分享标签页打开，结束时点击停止分享。

Which mode should I use?模式怎么选？

Local room本地房间
For friends on the same Wi-Fi or local network, such as at home or in the office. If several local invitation addresses appear, choose the network your friends use.适合同一 Wi-Fi 或局域网里的朋友，比如同屋或同办公室。出现多个局域网邀请地址时，选择与朋友相同的网络。
Public invite公网邀请
For friends on another network, even in another city. Send them a temporary invitation and keep the App running. Closing it ends the room and invitation.朋友在外地、和你不在同一网络时，用临时公网邀请加入。分享期间保持 App 运行，退出后房间和邀请失效。

Already have a Piik site? Keep the App running and connect from the site. 已有 Piik 站点？保持 App 运行，从站点连接即可。

Public invite relies on P2P media connections, without a media-server fallback. Some networks may open the page but fail to receive video.公网邀请依赖 P2P 媒体连接，不提供媒体服务器转发兜底；部分网络可能出现网页能打开、画面无法连接的情况。

Physical-device coverage for the macOS and Linux apps is still limited. Test reports are welcome on GitHub Issues. On macOS, native capture requires macOS 13+. On Linux, it needs additional system components. Browser capture is also available.macOS 和 Linux 版 Piik App 仍需更多实机测试，欢迎有设备的朋友试用并在 GitHub Issues 反馈。 macOS 原生采集需要 macOS 13 及以上；Linux 原生采集需要额外系统组件。也可以用浏览器采集。

03Use your group's Piik site使用已有的 Piik 站点Open in a browser, or connect with Piik App浏览器直接用，也能搭配 Piik App
  1. Get a Piik site address.准备一个站点地址。

    A Piik site runs on a server set up by you or someone in your group. Use its address, or try the public site at https://demo.piik.tv.Piik 站点由你或朋友部署在服务器上。使用你们已有的地址， 也可以先用公开站点 https://demo.piik.tv。

  2. Connect Piik App to the site.用 App 连接站点。

    Keep Piik App running and open the site in your browser. Click App at the top, check the address on the local confirmation page, then choose Allow access. Enter any site passphrase on the site itself.保持 Piik App 运行，在浏览器打开站点，点击顶部的 App。 在本机确认页核对网址，点击允许使用。如果站点要求口令，在站点页面输入。

    The same browser remembers this connection. On later visits, keep the App running; the page reconnects when you choose what to share. Allow local-network access if your browser asks. 同一浏览器会记住这次连接。之后保持 App 运行，直接访问站点， 选择分享来源时会自动连接 App。 浏览器询问本地网络权限时，请允许访问。

  3. Share a picture and invite your friends.选好画面，把邀请发给朋友。

    Select Start sharing and choose a source. Check the preview, then Copy invite link and send it to your friends. Keep the App and sharing tab open.点击开始分享并选择来源，确认预览后，复制邀请链接发给朋友。 分享期间保持 App 和分享标签页打开。

App and site, togetherApp 与站点，搭配着用

Use the App's window, screen and audio capture with your group's regular Piik address. Your friends can still watch in a browser.用上 App 的窗口、屏幕与声音采集，也保留站点的固定入口。朋友照样用浏览器观看。

With the site's media forwarding (SFU) enabled and the host's Privacy mode off, Piik can try this fallback when a direct connection is unavailable. demo.piik.tv is P2P-only; this fallback is not enabled there. 站点启用媒体转发（SFU）、房主关闭隐私模式后，直连不通时，Piik 会自动尝试这条备用路线。 demo.piik.tv 仅使用 P2P，未开启 SFU 兜底。

To share using just a browser, open the same site on a computer and select Start sharing. Available sources and audio depend on your browser and system. 也可以直接用电脑浏览器打开同一站点，点击开始分享。 可选画面和声音取决于浏览器与系统。

Here to watch a friend?来围观朋友的分享？

  1. Open your friend’s invitation in a phone or computer browser.用手机或电脑浏览器打开朋友发来的邀请链接。
  2. Wait for the picture. If playback does not start, select Play.等待画面出现；如果没有自动播放，点击播放。
  3. Move the pointer over the video or tap it to show volume, fullscreen and other playback controls.将鼠标移到画面上，或轻点画面，显示音量、全屏等播放控件。

Only have a four-digit room code? Ask your friend for the site address. Open it, select Join a room and enter the code. If the page asks for a passphrase or password, ask the host for it.如果朋友只发了四位房间号，先请他发来站点网址。打开网址，选加入房间并输入房间号。如果页面要求密码，请向房主索取。

Frequently asked questions常见问题

Documentation文档中心 Troubleshooting疑难解答
How do I share my screen with friends?怎么把我的屏幕分享给朋友？

Open Piik online, select Start sharing and choose a window or your whole screen. Once you see your preview, send friends the invitation link. They can watch in their browser without installing the App or creating an account. Share your screen in a browser

打开 Piik 在线版，点开始分享，选一个窗口或整个屏幕。看到预览后，把邀请链接发给朋友。朋友用浏览器打开就能看，无需安装 App 或注册账号。用浏览器分享屏幕

Which devices can I use?哪些设备可以用 Piik？

Friends can watch on a phone, tablet or computer. Piik App is available for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android. On iPhone and iPad, use Safari. Sharing the device screen requires iOS or iPadOS 27 or later, and game and video audio are unavailable during screen sharing. iPhone and iPad screen sharing

手机、平板和电脑都能用浏览器观看。Windows、macOS、Linux 和 Android 也有 Piik App。iPhone 和 iPad 用 Safari 观看。分享设备屏幕需要 iOS 或 iPadOS 27 及以上版本，分享时观众听不到游戏或视频的声音。iPhone 与 iPad 屏幕分享

When would I use the App instead of the browser?有了在线版，什么时候需要 App？

You can share, watch, chat and talk in a browser. Use Piik App to host a room on your device, let a friend control your computer, or share sound from one Windows app. To use these features on an existing site, start Piik App, select App at the top right of the site and approve the connection.

浏览器里就能分享、观看、聊天和通话。想用自己的设备开房间、让朋友操作你的电脑，或只分享某个 Windows 程序的声音时，可以用 Piik App。如果已经在一个 Piik 站点里，先启动 App，再点站点右上角的 App 并允许连接。

How many friends can join, and how do I invite them?一个房间能邀请多少人？怎么邀请？

One host can share with up to 20 viewers. Copy the full invitation link or show its QR code. Anyone who receives a password-free invitation can use it. If a friend has only the four-digit room code, send them your site address so they can select Join a room there and enter the code.

一个房间可以有 1 位房主和最多 20 位观众。可以复制完整的邀请链接，也可以让朋友扫邀请二维码。拿到免密邀请的人都能使用它。如果朋友只有四位房间号，再把你使用的站点网址发给他，让他打开后点加入房间并输入房间号。

Can my friends hear the game and talk to me?朋友能听到游戏声音，也能和我语音聊天吗？

Yes. When choosing a screen or window, turn on the sound option if one is available. For a browser game, share its tab and include tab audio. To talk to each other, open Interact → Voice and turn on your microphones. Missing game sound, quiet microphones and echo

可以。选屏幕或窗口时，如果有声音开关，把它打开。分享网页游戏时，可以选游戏所在的标签页并勾选分享标签页音频。想边看边聊，双方在互动 → 语音里加入并打开麦克风。游戏没声音、听不到朋友或有回声

Can a friend play the game on my computer?朋友能一起玩我电脑上的游戏吗？

Yes. Share your game from the desktop App, enable Remote control and accept your friend’s request. Games with local multiplayer can have several players. A friend on a phone can use the on-screen joystick. Remote control and playing together

可以。用电脑上的 Piik App 分享游戏，打开远程控制，同意朋友的控制请求。游戏支持本地多人模式时，几位朋友可以一起玩。用手机的朋友也可以打开屏幕摇杆。远程控制与多人同乐

Do I need a server to share with friends elsewhere?和异地的朋友分享，要自己搭服务器吗？

No. Use Piik online or select Public invite in Piik App to get a temporary internet address. For a permanent site for your group, you can host Piik on your own server. Deploy Piik Server

不用。可以使用在线版，也可以在 Piik App 里选公网邀请，生成一个临时网址发给朋友。如果想让大家长期使用同一个站点，可以自行部署。部署 Piik Server
