Linux capture dependencies (not bundled)

The Linux native capture process dynamically uses the distribution's XDG
Desktop Portal/libportal and GStreamer packages. Screener does not redistribute
those shared libraries. Their copyright notices and license texts remain the
responsibility of the distribution or package maintainer that supplies them.

libportal is distributed under LGPL-3.0-or-later. Source and license:
https://github.com/flatpak/libportal/blob/main/COPYING

GStreamer and its plugins carry the license and notices of each upstream
component. The core project is LGPL-2.1-or-later; consult the installed distro
packages for the exact plugin notices. Source and licensing information:
https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/documentation/frequently-asked-questions/licensing.html
https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/

The runtime stack also depends on the host's Portal, PipeWire, and hardware
encoder services. Those services are not part of the Screener Client package.
