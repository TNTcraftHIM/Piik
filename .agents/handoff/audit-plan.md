# Whole-tree check: the plan

Written by Claude; a queue attachment to [to-codex.md](./to-codex.md), deleted when it is absorbed.
A read-only pass over thirty-two units of the tree, each read in full and then refuted by a second
reader, merged into what follows. Nothing was built or run for it. Lines are those of the
revision each reader saw and may have moved.

Start with the history read. It has two existing consumers with demonstrably different needs, and the saving can be measured at the writer, on the wire, and at the reader. The next two changes with comparable reach are carrying a confirmed sequence advance through to the UI and sending a byte-delivery tree once from the room writer.

The 36 refutations contain **253 DO verdicts, 10 OWNER verdicts covering eight decisions, and 12 DO-NOT verdicts**. The plan merges repeat sightings. Effort is per change: **S** is under a day, **M** a few days, and **L** longer. “Internal” means the change affects no wire, stored format, or archive. Checks below are acceptance cases for implementation. This was a read-only review with no builds, tests, or network access.

Source lines use the refuters’ revisions. Rust filenames are qualified by crate where ambiguous. Linked refutations give the full producer and caller locations. I checked subsequent source changes through `e91e942d`. One material reduction has already landed and is identified below. The final worktree status was clean.
