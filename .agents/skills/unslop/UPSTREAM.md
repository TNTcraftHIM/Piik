The adaptation keeps product-specific direction, whole-screen hierarchy,
semantic grouping, aligned spacing, quiet chrome and rendered review. Piik's
existing visual and engineering standards own its controls, colours, typography,
motion, accessibility and independent activity lifetimes. The upstream's fixed
token values, broad style prohibitions, direction-approval pause and render-count
limit are not adopted. The method applies to material UI design and polish,
without turning ordinary implementation into a mandatory audit.

Upstream reference guides, examples, image assets, snapshot/story scripts and
their tool dependencies are not vendored. Source links above preserve access to
that material without making it part of Piik's required workflow.
